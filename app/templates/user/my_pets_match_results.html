Pet Compatibility Disclaimer

Informational Assessment Only

PetSona's pet owner compatibility assessment provides informational guidance based on lifestyle alignment, personality compatibility, and behavioral matching. Results are estimates only and should not be interpreted as guarantees of successful pet ownership.

No Guarantee of Compatibility

PetSona does not guarantee:

  • Behavioral compatibility
  • Health outcomes
  • Temperament consistency
  • Long-term owner satisfaction
  • Training success
  • Absence of behavioral issues

Every animal possesses unique personality traits, individual behaviors, and specific needs that may differ from generalized characteristics.

Professional Consultation

We strongly encourage you to:

  • Spend quality time with your pet
  • Consult with veterinarians and animal behaviorists
  • Research breed/species-specific information
  • Evaluate your long-term capacity and commitment

Individual Responsibility

You remain fully responsible for all aspects of pet ownership, including care decisions, behavioral management, healthcare, and financial obligations.
